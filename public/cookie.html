Cookie Policy

Effective Date: November 19, 2026 | Website: https://gta6vicedb.com

This Cookie Policy explains how VICE.DB ("we," "our," or "us") uses cookies and browser local storage technologies when you visit https://gta6vicedb.com.

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies are small text files stored on your browser by websites you visit. Similarly, HTML5 localStorage allows web applications to save functional preferences directly on your device without transmitting data to external servers.

2. Types of Storage and Cookies We Use

  • Essential Functional Storage (Local Storage): Used to remember your GTA 6 Interactive Map checklist progress (discovered vehicle spawns, weapons, and collectibles) across sessions without requiring user login.
  • Analytics Cookies: We use tools such as Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate traffic patterns, popular vehicle searches, and page load performance.
  • Advertising Cookies (Google AdSense): Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies (such as the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to https://gta6vicedb.com or other websites.

3. Managing and Opting Out of Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. To opt out of personalized advertising by Google, visit Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info. Please note that clearing browser site data will reset your saved map checklist progress.

4. Updates & Contact

We may update this Cookie Policy to reflect regulatory or technical changes. For questions, reach out via our Contact Page or email contact@gta6vicedb.com.
