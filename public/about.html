About VICE.DB

VICE.DB is an independent, unofficial fan site for Grand Theft Auto VI. It combines a vehicle database (prices, real-life counterparts, performance, free spawn locations) with an interactive map of Leonida. It is not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

Who runs it

VICE.DB is run by a GTA player who plays GTA 6 on Xbox Series X|S. The site is maintained by one person, so corrections from readers are very welcome.

Where the data comes from

  • Our own play. Prices, spawn locations and performance notes are checked in the game. Vehicle images are our own in-game screenshots, taken in photo mode.
  • Official sources. Rockstar Newswire announcements, trailers and screenshots, credited where used.
  • Community reports. Information from other players and wikis (text adapted under CC BY-SA with attribution to GTA Wiki).

Verified vs. unverified

Every vehicle and map pin carries a status. Verified means we confirmed it in-game ourselves or found it in at least two reliable sources. Unverified entries come from trailers, previews or single reports and are shown with a warning until we can check them. Performance ratings are on a 0-100 scale and are estimates until marked verified.

Corrections

Found a wrong price, a missing car or a spawn location that has moved? Send it through the contact page. We usually update the database within a few days.
