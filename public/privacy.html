Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 19, 2026 | Website: https://gta6vicedb.com

VICE.DB ("we," "our," or "us") operates https://gta6vicedb.com. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you visit our Grand Theft Auto VI interactive map and vehicle database.

1. Information We Collect

Browser Local Storage: To save your interactive map progress (discovered collectibles, weapons, and vehicle spawn pins) without account registration, VICE.DB stores checklist data locally in your browser's localStorage. This data stays on your device.

Log Data & Analytics: Our hosting provider (Netlify) and analytics tools (Google Analytics 4) automatically collect standard log information including IP address, browser type, device type, and page views to improve site performance.

2. Cookies and Google AdSense Advertising

VICE.DB uses third-party advertising companies, including Google AdSense, to serve ads. Google's use of advertising cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to https://gta6vicedb.com and other websites. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

3. GDPR & CCPA Privacy Rights

Visitors from the European Economic Area (GDPR) and California residents (CCPA/CPRA) have the right to access, delete, or opt out of data processing and personalized ad cookies via browser settings or consent controls.

4. Third-Party Links & Children's Privacy

We are not responsible for external websites linked on our pages. VICE.DB does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.
